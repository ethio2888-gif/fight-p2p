🥊 About Fight P2P

Welcome to Fight P2P, a premium peer-to-peer boxing match prediction platform designed for sports enthusiasts who love strategic thinking and friendly competition.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to create a safe, entertaining, and skill-based environment where boxing fans can test their knowledge, engage with their favorite organizations, and connect with fellow sports lovers — all using a virtual points system.

🌟 What Makes Us Different

  • Peer-to-Peer Model: Players predict outcomes against each other, not against the house.
  • Virtual Points Only: No real money is involved. Points are purely for entertainment.
  • Ethiopian Boxing Focus: We proudly support local boxing organizations like ETFC and international federations.
  • Transparent System: All predictions, results, and payouts are publicly visible.
  • Community-Driven: Built by fans, for fans of the sweet science.

🏢 Organizations We Feature

Fight P2P showcases upcoming matches from various boxing organizations. Whether it's a local Ethiopian event or an international championship, you'll find it here.

🔒 Commitment to Responsible Play

Although our platform uses only virtual points, we encourage all users to:

  • Play responsibly and for fun only
  • Be at least 18 years old to register
  • Maintain a healthy balance between entertainment and daily life
